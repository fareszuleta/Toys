Product safety standards

All products undergo third-party testing before listing. We work with Bureau Veritas Consumer Products Services (Shenzhen and Portland labs) to verify compliance with international toy safety standards.

ASTM F963-23

Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Toy Safety, published by ASTM International. Covers mechanical/physical hazards, flammability, chemical properties, and labeling for toys sold in the United States.

EN71-1:2014+A1:2018

Safety of Toys — Part 1: Mechanical and Physical Properties. European standard covering sharp points, edges, choking hazards, and structural integrity. Required for toys sold in the EU/EEA.

CPSIA Compliance

Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act. All products meet CPSC requirements for total lead content, lead in surface coatings, and phthalate content in children's toys.

Age labeling

Products labeled 0+ are suitable from birth. Products labeled 3+ contain components per ASTM F963 small parts requirements. Each product listing specifies its age recommendation.
