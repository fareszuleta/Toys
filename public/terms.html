1. About Playroom Toys

Playroom Toys, LLC is a limited liability company registered with the Oregon Secretary of State (Business Registry #1234782) and operating under Portland Business License BL-2019-048721. Our principal office is at 2847 NW Everett St, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97210. We have been in continuous operation since 2019.

We sell curated toys for children ages 0–10. All products undergo third-party safety testing by Bureau Veritas Consumer Products Services against ASTM F963-23 (US) and EN71-1:2014+A1:2018 (EU) standards prior to listing. Test reports are available upon request at hello@playroom-toys.com.

2. Payments & Security

All payments are processed exclusively through Stripe, Inc., a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. When you enter your card details during checkout, the data is transmitted directly to Stripe's servers using TLS 1.3 encryption. Playroom Toys never receives, stores, processes, or has access to your full card number or CVC.

We receive only: card brand (e.g., Visa), last four digits, and expiration date — solely for displaying your saved payment method in your account. You may remove a saved card at any time from your account settings.

3. Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and hashed password (scrypt with random salt)
  • Shipping information: address you provide at checkout, stored for order fulfillment
  • Order history: items purchased, order totals, and delivery status
  • Payment reference: Stripe payment method ID, card brand, last four digits, and expiration (never the full card number)

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Full card numbers, CVCs, or PINs (handled exclusively by Stripe)
  • Social Security numbers or government IDs
  • Browsing activity on other websites
  • Location data beyond the shipping address you provide

Data Sharing

We share your information only with: (1) Stripe, for payment processing; (2) UPS and USPS, for shipping fulfillment; (3) law enforcement, when required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Shipping Policy

We ship from our Portland, OR fulfillment center via UPS Ground and USPS Priority Mail. Tracking is provided by email within 4 hours of dispatch.

  • Standard shipping (US): $4.95, 2–4 business days. Free on orders over $60.
  • Express shipping (US): $12.95, 2 business days.
  • International (CA, MX, Latin America): 5–10 business days via tracked courier.

5. Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for items in original, unopened condition. Return shipping is free for US customers. To initiate a return, contact us at hello@playroom-toys.com or call (503) 741-2988. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item.

6. Product Safety

Every product listed on Playroom Toys has been tested by Bureau Veritas Consumer Products Services and meets or exceeds the following standards:

  • ASTM F963-23 — Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Toy Safety (United States)
  • EN71-1:2014+A1:2018 — Safety of Toys, Part 1: Mechanical and Physical Properties (European Union)
  • CPSIA — Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act compliance for lead and phthalate content

Products labeled 0+ are suitable from birth. Products labeled 3+ contain components that may present a choking hazard for children under 3 years, in compliance with ASTM F963 small parts requirements. Each product listing specifies its age recommendation.
